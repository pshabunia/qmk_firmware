## TL;DR

This build uses the trackpoint module `WZ-T420v1.1` that normally comes embedded into the USB circuit board with a whimsical label `FDK-TEST`. 

It's been the most afordable and available module on the market for many years. It's easy to find at online marketplaces: Amazon, Walmart, eBay and Aliexpress. The tricky part is that wiring this module is not easy. It requires both patience and steady hand to resolder the module without overheating the capacitors which are placed too close to pins. Covering sensitive elements on the board with a few layers of tape while soldering is always a good idea.

The module needs to be connected to microcontroller via PS/2 curcuit. The process is well explained in [QMK docs](https://docs.qmk.fm/features/ps2_mouse) and other build logs - see credits at the top of the page. The module's pinout is published on the [Deskthority wiki](https://deskthority.net/wiki/TrackPoint_Hardware#USB_Trackpoint_WZ-T420).
